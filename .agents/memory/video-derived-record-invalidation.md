---
name: Video-derived record invalidation
description: Preserving privacy boundaries for Coach-only development records tied to private video evidence.
---

Evidence-backed development records are not independent of the underlying private video session. A source session becoming unapproved, unconsented, or no longer actively retained must suppress every derivative that cites it, even for the assigned Coach.

**Why:** Creation-time validation alone leaves stale coaching content retrievable after consent withdrawal or retention cleanup, defeating the source video's privacy boundary.

**How to apply:** New evidence-linked records should retain a link to an approved session or revision and guarded reads should revalidate its current eligibility. Multi-source outputs must be hidden when any cited source is no longer eligible; records without video evidence may remain available only when their purpose is independently valid.
